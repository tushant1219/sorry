FOR MY MADAM JI ♡

Meri Pyari Madam Ji 🎀❤️

Dear Madam Ji, meri pyari Malkin ❤️👑🫶🏻

Sabse pehle, main tumse dil se sorry kehna chahta hoon. Mujhe pata hai ki mujhse galtiyan hui hain. Kabhi-kabhi main bahut zyada overthink karne lagta hoon, possessive aur overprotective ho jaata hoon, aur apni feelings samjhate-samjhate aisi baatein keh deta hoon jo tumhe hurt karti hain. Main apni galtiyon ke liye koi bahana nahi banana chahta. Mujhe sach mein afsos hai ki meri wajah se tumhe takleef hui. 🥺❤️‍🩹

Jab tumne mujhe milne ke liye bulaya tha aur main nahi aa paaya, toh mujhe tumhari feelings ko aur achhe se samajhna chahiye tha. Main chahta tha ki hum apne career aur relationship ko saath lekar chalein, lekin shayad apni baat samjhane ki koshish mein main tumhari baat dhyaan se sunna bhool gaya. Mujhe samajhna chahiye tha ki tumhari feelings aur tumhari expectations bhi utni hi important hain. 💗

September se lekar ab tak, agar meri kisi baat, gusse ya behaviour ne tumhara dil dukhaya hai, toh uske liye main dil se maafi maangta hoon. Main beete hue waqt ko badal nahi sakta, lekin apni galtiyon ko samajhkar khud ko behtar banane ki koshish zaroor kar sakta hoon. Main seekhna chahta hoon ki gusse mein kaise rukna hai, overthinking ko kaise control karna hai aur tumhari baat ko bina kisi behas ke kaise sunna hai. ❤️‍🩹🫂

Madam Ji, tum mere liye bahut special ho. Tumhari smile, tumhari khushi aur tumhara sukoon mere liye bahut maayne rakhte hain. 👑🎀 Lekin main yeh bhi samajhta hoon ki kisi ki care karne ka matlab us par apni baat maanna zaroori banana nahi hota. Pyaar mein trust, understanding, respect aur ek-doosre ko space dena bhi utna hi zaroori hai.

Main sirf sorry bolkar sab kuch theek hone ki umeed nahi rakhna chahta. Main apne actions se behtar insaan banna chahta hoon. Main chahta hoon ki meri baaton se nahi, balki mere behaviour se tumhe meri sincerity nazar aaye. Main jaanta hoon ki trust dobara banne mein waqt lag sakta hai, aur main is baat ko samajhta hoon. 🥹🌷

Please yaar, agar kabhi tumhare dil mein mere liye maafi ki jagah ho, toh mujhe maaf kar dena. 😭❤️ Main tumhe khona nahi chahta, lekin main tum par apne saath rehne ya mujhe maaf karne ka pressure bhi nahi daalna chahta. Tumhari feelings, tumhara sukoon aur tumhara decision mere liye respect ke layak hain.

Main tumse bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki jo kuch bhi hua, uske liye mujhe dil se afsos hai. Kaash main pehle hi apni galtiyon ko samajh paata aur tumhe hurt karne se khud ko rok paata. Main apni galtiyon ki responsibility leta hoon aur khud mein sachcha badlaav laane ki koshish karunga.

Agar kabhi tum khud mujhse baat karna chaho, toh main tumhari baat calmly sununga aur tumhari feelings ko samajhne ki poori koshish karunga. Aur agar tumhe waqt aur space chahiye, toh main uski bhi respect karunga. ❤️🦋

Apna khayal rakhna, Madam Ji. Hamesha khush rehna aur apni pyari si smile banaye rakhna. 🎀🌸

Ek baar phir, meri taraf se dil se sorry. Main perfect nahi hoon, lekin apni galtiyon ko pehchaan kar behtar banna chahta hoon.

With love, care and a sincere apology,
Tumhara pagal sa insaan ❤️🥹🫶🏻
Always wishing you happiness ♡
